# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-26

YOLO-Master for iOS ("the app") is an on-device object detection app. This
policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to that information.

## Summary

The app runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or store
your personal data on any server. There is no backend that receives your data.

## What the app accesses

- Camera: used only for live, on-device detection in the Live tab. Camera
  frames are processed in memory and are never saved or transmitted.
- Photo Library: used only to read images you explicitly choose for the Photo
  tab, and to save detection frames that you explicitly export. The app does not
  access your library otherwise.

All inference runs locally on the Apple Neural Engine, GPU, or CPU.

## Data collection

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data, images, detection
results, analytics, or usage data. The app contains no third-party analytics or
advertising components.

## Data you send to us

The only way we receive anything from you is if you choose to contact us or
report an issue yourself, for example by opening an issue on the project's GitHub
repository. In that case we receive only what you send.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
